# Hero developer illustration

The owner approved the developer-at-laptop preview and requested its return to the original inline position after “I build digital products” in the desktop hero headline. The full illustration scales with the heading font size, with the existing mobile layout preserved. It is decorative and excluded from the accessibility tree.

Generated using the built-in `image_gen` tool. The approved illustration was edited only to empty the eye whites; DOM pupils restore the dark eyes and highlights and provide bounded cursor tracking. GSAP motion respects reduced motion and fine-pointer preferences and cleans up on unmount. The character has a brief, gentle tilt on pointer entry.

The two pupils share a 512 × 512 SVG coordinate system with calibrated neutral centers and elliptical shapes. Both follow one gaze vector, with smaller travel for the farther eye; the eyelid clips stay fixed. Cursor coordinates use the SVG screen transform so responsive sizing, ancestor reveals, and hover rotation do not introduce different directions for the two eyes. Percentage translations inside independently rotated eye masks must not be reintroduced.

The transparent image is resized with Sharp to a 512 × 512 WebP (about 45 KB), bundled at `public/images/hero/developer.webp`. The former duck image is retained as an unused design asset; the homepage now renders `components/hero-developer.tsx`.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: preview illustration of a tiny inline character for the headline of an existing dark software engineer portfolio. Primary request: one friendly developer sitting and coding at an open laptop. Create a charming polished 3D clay/rubber-toy style illustration with softly rounded forms, a slightly oversized head, short dark hair, warm natural skin, a mild focused smile, and clearly visible eyes looking down toward the screen. The developer wears a charcoal casual top, with hands naturally resting on the laptop keyboard. Show a compact seated upper-body pose in a gentle three-quarter view facing slightly LEFT toward the surrounding headline. Laptop is lime green #c7f36b, with a simple dark, unbranded lid. Let a small portion of a restrained chair indicate sitting; keep the composition compact and readable at approximately 70px tall. Soft studio lighting, warm ivory highlights, subtle dimensional shading, clean crisp silhouette. Square composition centered, all of the head, hands and laptop inside the canvas, 12 percent transparent padding around the silhouette. Genuinely transparent alpha background outside the character and laptop: no room, scenery, floor, background panel or desk spanning the canvas. No text, logos, emoji, watermark, extra characters, plants, coffee cups or decorative clutter. This is a standalone asset preview, not a website screenshot or mockup.
